# Problem brief

## User

Initial design partner hypothesis: an Iranian FMCG brand, distributor, or multi-store retailer that evaluates promotions in spreadsheets.

## Problem

Observed sales lift after a discount does not equal incremental demand or incremental profit. It may include baseline demand, substitution from another SKU, forward-buy, seasonality, distribution changes, or stockout artifacts.

## First promise

For the public MVP, use a documented weekly sales/price/promotion panel to produce an auditable
promotion-lift analysis with uncertainty and an explicit decision to approve, reject, or run a
better experiment. Profit is unavailable until real cost data is supplied by a design partner.

## Non-goals for v0

- Full revenue-growth-management suite
- Autonomous execution of discounts
- Customer-level personalization
- Causal claims when overlap or pre-trend checks fail
